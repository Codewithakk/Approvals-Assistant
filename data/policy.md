## Chunk 1 — Review SLA
All pending approvals must receive a first review within one business day of submission. Items that block live operations (onboarding folders, active patrol checklists) are treated as Priority 1 and reviewed immediately. Reference-only items such as PDF specs are Priority 3 and may wait until the end of the day.

## Chunk 2 — Escalation
If an approval has been pending for more than 24 hours without action, it must be escalated to the shift supervisor. Escalations are logged in the approvals dashboard and reviewed in the daily handover. Do not escalate items that were submitted outside business hours until the next shift begins.

## Chunk 3 — Approval authority
Operators may approve items they own. Items submitted by another operator require a second reviewer at the same level or above. Folder and Video items always require a second reviewer because they change live patrol behavior. Image items may be approved solo if they are metadata-only.

## Chunk 4 — Rejection and rework
Rejections must include a one-line reason visible to the submitter. The submitter has 48 hours to resubmit with changes. After two rejections, the item is frozen and requires supervisor override.

## Chunk 5 — Audit
Every approval, rejection, and escalation is audited. Audit records are immutable and retained for 12 months. Operators can view their own audit trail; supervisors can view the full trail.
